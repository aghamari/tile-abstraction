The number is the offset

          

            Same idea as CuTe print_layout. Rows and columns are
            the coordinate. The number in the cell is where that coordinate
            sits in memory.
          

        

        

          
Which side has stride 1

          

            Row-major (5, 5):(5, 1) walks across a row.
            Column-major (5, 5):(1, 5) walks down a column.
            Cell (2, 3) is 13 in the first and 17 in the second.
            The strip under the grid is that same offset in address order.
          

          

            (2, 4):(12, 1) is the strided row from that page.
            The row is still contiguous. Eight addresses are skipped before
            the next row, and the strip shows those gaps.
          

        

        

          
Nested, then the button

          

            (2, (2, 2)):(4, (2, 1)) and
            (3, (2, 3)):(3, (12, 1)) are the nested prints on the
            CuTe layout page. The layout stays up. The offsets stay blank
            until Show the indices.
          

          

            The reverse tab fills the table first. The layout stays hidden
            until Show the layout.
          

          

            The tiled tab and the overlapping tab show the picture with the
            offsets filled in. The shape stays in the layout line. The four
            strides start empty. Check says Okay, or Not okay.
          

          

            Partition S shows the layout first. Show the tiles draws the six
            4×8 tiles. The (4, 2) is eight columns of one row,
            two copies of four, not a 4×2 block.
          

          

            The last quiz is partition_S on a 30×16 block. The
            print is ((4, 5), 3, 2):((1, 16), 160, 8). The 4 is
            columns, the 5 is rows, the 3 is tiles down, and the 2 is tiles
            across.
          

          

            The transpose quiz is one ds_read_tr. Source and dest
            print the same shape, ((4, 2), 2, 3). The stride of
            the 4 is 24 on the source and 1 on the dest. Show the answer draws
            both.
          

          

            Layout A shows one 16×16 tile. The top number is the lane. The
            bottom number is that lane's value. A column is contiguous, so the
            next column starts 16 later. Show the answer prints
            ((16, 4), 4):((1, 64), 16).
          

          

            Retile shows one coordinate in two places. The tensor row is 16
            wide, so the next group of 16 rows starts at 256. The same group
            in the registers starts 4 later.
